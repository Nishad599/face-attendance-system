About this system

Contactless attendance by face recognition. Students are marked present by looking at a camera — no cards and no roll-calls. Face data is held only on this server and never sent anywhere else. See the privacy notice for what is stored, why, and for how long.

Attendance is recorded Monday to Saturday, excluding declared holidays.
